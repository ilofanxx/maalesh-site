Support

Questions or problems? Email support@nomaalesh.com. We reply within two working days.

Maalesh is provided by PNEUMA GENERAL TRADING L.L.C - O.P.C, Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates.

Cancel a subscription

Open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Maalesh. It stays active until the paid period ends.

Delete your account

In the app: the profile button on the first page, Settings and support, Delete my account. No subscription is needed. If you cannot sign in, email us from the same address.

Report content or a person

Press and hold a message and choose Report or Block. Stories have both buttons at the top. To report a name or photo, open the chat menu. We review reports within 24 hours.
